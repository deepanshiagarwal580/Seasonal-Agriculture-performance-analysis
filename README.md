# Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes agricultural data across different seasons to identify meaningful patterns, trends, relationships, and variations in agricultural performance.

The analysis focuses on seasonal differences in agricultural yield, production, economic performance, environmental conditions, resource usage, water efficiency, and crop-wise performance.

## Objectives

- Explore and understand the agricultural dataset
- Clean and prepare the data for analysis
- Compare agricultural performance across seasons
- Analyze environmental conditions and resource usage
- Examine water efficiency across irrigation methods
- Compare crop performance across seasons
- Identify relationships between environmental/resource factors and agricultural performance
- Identify significant and unusual patterns
- Develop evidence-based conclusions and recommendations

## Dataset

The dataset contains 4,000 agricultural observations covering three seasons:

- Kharif
- Rabi
- Zaid

It includes information related to agricultural practices, environmental conditions, crop production, resource usage, and economic performance.

## Key Findings

- Kharif recorded the highest average yield among the three seasons.
- Kharif also recorded the highest average production and profit.
- Zaid recorded comparatively lower average yield and production.
- Zaid showed a negative average profit in the dataset.
- Environmental conditions such as rainfall, temperature, humidity and soil moisture varied across seasons.
- Water usage and water efficiency varied across seasons and irrigation methods.
- Crop-wise analysis showed differences in yield across seasons, with Kharif recording higher average yield than Rabi and Zaid for all crops in the dataset.
